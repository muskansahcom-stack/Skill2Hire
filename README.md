# 🚀 Skill2Hire

### From Skills to Opportunities

Skill2Hire is an AI-powered, skill-first career ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between a student's current skills and the right career opportunities.

Instead of simply helping students search and apply for jobs, Skill2Hire focuses on understanding their skills, identifying skill gaps, helping them improve, and connecting them with relevant opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle to understand which skills they are missing, what they should learn next, and which career opportunities actually match their abilities.

At the same time, recruiters face challenges in finding relevant candidates among a large number of applications.

Skill2Hire addresses this gap by connecting skill development with career opportunities.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Skill2Hire provides a complete journey:

**Skills → Skill Gap → Learning → Practice → Assessment → Job Readiness → Opportunity Matching → Apply → Hire**

The platform helps students understand where they are, what they are missing, and what they should do next.

---

## 🔥 Key Innovation

Our core innovation is the **Skill-First Approach**.

Traditional platforms generally follow:

**Job → Search → Apply**

Skill2Hire follows:

**Student → Skills → Skill Gap → Improve → Validate → Match → Apply**

The platform doesn't only help students find opportunities; it helps them become prepared for the right opportunities.
